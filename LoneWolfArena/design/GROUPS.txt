GROUPS AND ALL-HUMAN TEAM MATCHES (1.5.0) - the contract for the two builders (CORE, UI)
==========================================================================================

Owner's request (2026-11-06): LONE WOLF online never brings bots; matchmaking finds real players.
A player invites friends into a GROUP in the lobby; a member's character stands next to theirs
in the lobby; a member's phone shows EXIT GROUP; a group of 4 plays DUO 2 VS 2 among themselves.
TRAINING GROUND keeps its bots (offline, unchanged).

1. WHAT THE PLAYER SEES
-----------------------
- GROUP (lobby, LONE WOLF): up to GROUP_MAX = 4 players. The first invite makes the inviter the
  LEADER. The TEAM bar becomes the GROUP bar: the leader's "+ INVITE" lists online friends
  (Cloud.peers ∩ friends, never blocked ones); an invited friend sees GROUP INVITE "<NAME> invites
  you to their group" with ACCEPT / DECLINE / BLOCK; ACCEPT puts them in the group (no match starts).
- Each member's character (CharacterModel with their outfit bundle) stands on the lobby podium
  beside the player's own (up to 3 more, smaller, left/right of the hero), with a name plate and a
  LEADER mark on the leader. Every phone in the group shows the same members.
- A member's phone shows EXIT GROUP (leaves; the others see them go). The leader's phone shows
  DISBAND GROUP (everyone leaves) and, per member, REMOVE. Only the leader's START works; a member
  sees "WAITING FOR <LEADER> TO START" instead of START (SELECT MODE follows the leader's choice).
- START (leader), LONE WOLF online, NO BOTS EVER:
  - 1 VS 1 (SOLO): unchanged QUICK MATCH (already bot-free). A group cannot start 1 VS 1 (the
    START notice says to EXIT GROUP or pick DUO / SQUAD).
  - DUO (2 vs 2, 4 humans):
      group of 4 -> the match starts at once among them: BLUE = leader + first member who joined,
                    ORANGE = the other two;
      group of 2 or solo -> matchmaking: "SEARCHING PLAYERS 3/4" until 4 humans in two teams of 2
                    (a group of 2 is always one team); CANCEL any time. No timer, no bots.
      group of 3 -> START says "DUO needs a group of 1, 2 or 4".
  - SQUAD (4 vs 4, 8 humans): matchmaking the same way ("PLAYERS n/8"), a group (1-4) always on
    one team. No bots.
- After a match every phone goes back to the lobby; the GROUP stays together (the leader presses
  START again: this replaces PLAY AGAIN TOGETHER for groups). The results screen of an all-human
  match shows LOBBY (no REMATCH / PLAY AGAIN TOGETHER).

2. WIRE PROTOCOL (all under Cloud.ROOT = "lonewolfarena/v1")
------------------------------------------------------------
Group room: ROOT/g/<gid> (gid 32 hex), packets exactly like match rooms (_room_packet: version 1,
IV, AES-256-CBC JSON with "f" sender id and "n" counter per sender, HMAC-SHA256), with the group's
own AES and HMAC keys. A receiver keeps a counter per sender (replays dropped) and accepts only
current members (plus a "gjoin" from an id the leader invited).
  "ginv" (inbox ROOT/u/<id>, like "tinv"): {"t":"ginv","from","name","bundle","sec","pk","sig"}
        sec = RSA(invitee pk, gid(16) + aes(32) + mac(32) + size byte), sig over "ginv|from|to|sec".
        Shown only from a friend this phone takes challenges from, while the lobby listens; not in a
        match, not already in a group (busy -> "dec" with the invite's tag).
  group room messages (k):
        "gjoin"   {name, bundle}                      the invitee after ACCEPT (every 1 s until "groster")
        "groster" {members:[[id,name,bundle],...], mode, size, status}   leader, on every change and
                                                      every GROUP_BEAT_S (3 s): the truth for everyone
        "gbeat"   {}                                  each member every GROUP_BEAT_S; a member silent
                                                      GROUP_TIMEOUT_MS (12 s) is dropped by the leader;
                                                      the leader silent 12 s = the group is gone
        "gleave"  {}                                  EXIT GROUP; the leader drops them
        "gkick"   {id}                                leader: REMOVE
        "gend"    {}                                  leader: DISBAND (or the leader EXITs)
        "gstart"  {room, aes, mac, size, roster}      leader: the group's own 2 VS 2 / the merged
                                                      match (see below): every member joins it
Matchmaking (never retained): ROOT/mm/g<size> ("g2" DUO, "g4" SQUAD), every SEEK_S from each
searching LEADER (a solo searcher is a leader of 1): {"t":"gseek","id","n":members,"saw":[ids]}.
The searching leader with the lowest id among those it sees becomes the MATCH HOST: it picks
units (groups whole, never split) until 2 x size humans fit two teams, then sends each other unit's
leader a signed "gmatch" (inbox, RSA-wrapped like "ginv"): room, aes, mac, size, roster (slot ->
id, name, bundle, team). That leader answers "gacc"/"dec" (tagged) and forwards "gstart" in its
group room; every member subscribes the match room and says "join". A unit that took another
host's "gmatch" stops seeking. The host waits up to 15 s for every unit's "gacc"; a missing unit
is dropped and the host goes back to seeking with what remains.
Match room (all-human): ROOT/r/<room>, the 1 vs 1 packets, every member a sender. "join" every 1 s
until the host has heard all; the host then sends "begin" (Arena._net_setup's) to everyone. Events
and states are as today but delivered with the sender id; "bye" from a member = that slot LEFT; the
host's "bye" or silence = the match ended ("left").

4. MATCH (CORE): TeamArenaNet with many humans
----------------------------------------------
- TeamArena.link_roster(size, roster, my_id, host_id, seed) -> team_match dict (like link_match;
  "bot" false for every human slot; no bot slots in an all-human match).
- Each phone runs its own slot; every other human is a RemoteFighter driven by state_from(sender).
  The host is the referee (knocks, eliminations, revives, rounds) exactly as now; every guest
  reports its own "dn" / "rvd"; "hit" goes to the phone that runs the target (the target slot's
  owner applies it); decisions are broadcast. The limits of today's guest checks apply per sender
  (a sender may only speak for its own slot).
- A member who leaves (member_left) is LEFT for the rest of the match (TeamReferee.leave).
- Results: an all-human match records like a team match (HISTORY "DUO · WITH <teammate>").
- Direct UDP is off in all-human matches (relay only) for now; LAN is 1 vs 1 only.

6. CUSTOM ROOM WITH SLOTS (1.5.2, Cloud.open_room(pin, rules, code, size))
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
A room is a group (its own ROOT/g/<gid> room and keys, the host its leader) whose players joined by
code + password instead of an invite. size: SOLO 1 (2 places), DUO 2 (4), SQUAD 4 (8); team t's
places are roster slots t * NetGroup.per_team(size) .. + NetGroup.places(size, true) - 1.
  "ask" (ROOT/c/<code>, HMAC with the password's key) -> the host's "rin" carries "sec" =
        RSA(asker pk, gid + aes + mac + size byte) and "rt" = HMAC(password key, "rin" + sec + host);
        a full room answers "rno" {"full": true}; a KICKed id is never answered again.
  then the group room as above ("gjoin", the host's "groster" the only truth) plus
  "gslot"   {t, s}                                 a player asks for an empty place (Cloud.room_take)
START is the host's only (Cloud.room_start): SOLO = the 1 vs 1 with the room's rules
(start_room_match), DUO / SQUAD = _group_launch: the all-human match of the players in their places
(uneven allowed, empty places stay empty, no bots). The room closes when its match starts. A player
out of the room (gkick, gend, the host silent) gets Cloud.room_notice, shown once by the lobby.
