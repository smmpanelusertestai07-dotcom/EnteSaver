# Interfaces between the game's parts (keep these stable)

Owners: `arena.gd`, `fighter.gd`, `player.gd`, `bot.gd`, `weapons.gd`, `character_model.gd` are the
gameplay core. HUD, map and menus are separate files that talk to the core only through what is
listed here. If a part needs something new from the core, add it in its own file or note it.

## Arena (scripts/arena.gd) -> read by HUD / menus
- `phase` (Phase.PREP=0, FIGHT=1, END=2 (match over), BREAK=3 (between rounds)), `prep_left` (s),
  `time_left` (s), `elapsed` (s, this round), `match_time` (s), `round_index`, `rounds_to_win`
  (`Game.rounds_to_win(online)`: 5, a custom room's own 1-7 online, LW_ROUNDS_TO_WIN in test
  builds), `score` {"player": int, "bot": int}, `picked_weapon` (id),
  `pick_note` (prep line: who picks the gun, or which it is)
- the match's mode: `mode` ("training" | "lonewolf", a key of `Game.MODE_NAMES`), `mode_name`
  (what the HUD, scoreboard, pause menu and results show: TRAINING GROUND against the bot or the
  target dummy, LONE WOLF online), `practice` (the target dummy: endless HP, no zone, the clock
  counts up, nothing recorded), `online` (the opponent is another phone)
- zone: `zone_state` ("stationary" | "shrinking" | "closed"), `zone_next()` (s until it
  changes), `zone_radius`, `zone_center` (Vector3), `in_zone(pos)`, `zone_warning` (text or "")
- `fighters` (Array[Fighter]), `player`, `bot`, `map_name` ("CARGO CAGE"), `map_rect` (Rect2,
  world X/Z area of the minimap), `minimap_texture` (Texture2D rendered once from above, may be
  null for the first frames), `obstacles` (Array[Rect2] footprints, X/Z)
- `scoreboard_rows()` -> [{name, me, alive, hp, kills, damage, headshots, accuracy, rounds, weapon}]
- `open_pause()`; the HUD emits `pause_pressed` for it. `record_left()`: the pause menu's QUIT
  MATCH puts a counted match into the profile as LEFT, a defeat (HISTORY result "left"), with what
  was played so far; a match already decided keeps its own result.
- An online session that ends before the match (Cloud `session_ended(reason)`): "left" (the other
  phone said bye or went silent) is a forfeit win once the first fight began; "connection lost"
  (this phone lost the relay, or was away about as long as the other waits: `Cloud.AWAY_MS`) is a
  LEFT as with QUIT MATCH. Either way a match whose first fight had not begun counts nowhere; those
  go back to the lobby, which shows `Game.match_notice` ([title, text]) once. What the other
  phone's events may make here is capped (`spawn_cap(kind)`: its walls and frags per round).
- Online the HOST decides every round: each phone reports its own player's death ("died":
  [headshot, cause, round], cause `Arena.death_cause()`: "gun" / "frag" (the other's kill: the
  only kill credit), "zone", "fall", "self" (its own frag), "left"); the host decides `DEATH_SETTLE_S` after the first
  death it learns of (both down by then: a draw) or at its time-out (higher HP), and sends "rend"
  [round, winner, host score, guest score, timed out, headshot, killer]; the guest shows exactly
  that (score, banner, the match's end) and starts each round on the host's "begin" / "round",
  one-way trip earlier (`peer_rtt` / 2, or `relay_rtt` before that is measured). The host takes
  that trip off the guest's death reports, so "first one down" is fair. A forfeit win keeps the
  real score (no round added). The match ends when `Arena.match_over_after(round, mine, theirs,
  goal)` says so: first to `goal`, or after `Arena.MAX_ROUNDS` (9) rounds at most, drawn rounds
  counted (the most rounds won wins, level is a DRAW). At its end each phone sends "fin" [round,
  time, host HP, guest HP, host rounds, guest rounds, kills, damage, headshots, shots, hits] (time,
  HP and rounds from the host only, else -1; `Arena.final_from` checks it): the results wait for
  it (`FINAL_WAIT_S`), the opponent's row shows its own phone's numbers and the guest the host's
  TIME and HP LEFT, so both tables match; the host's "fin" also ends a guest match that still
  waited for a next round.
- Cloud: a frame gap while a match loads or in the first `Cloud.LOAD_STALL_MS` after its arena
  listens is the load (shader compiles), not this phone away (`Cloud.load_stall`); an app pause
  still counts. The other phone, told by "ka" keep-alives that it loads, waits that much longer.

## Arena -> HUD calls (scripts/hud.gd must implement all of these)
- `bind(player: Player, arena: Node)`, `reload_layout()`, `release_all()`
- `show_center(text, seconds, color := Color.WHITE, size := 96, sub := "")`
- `show_round_banner(round_index: int, area: String)`  (blue->orange band "ROUND 1 / AREA: ...")
- `open_weapon_pick(options: Array[String] weapon ids, preselect: String, seconds: float, on_pick: Callable(id))`
  must call `on_pick` exactly once (tap SELECT, or the timer runs out -> current choice);
  `close_weapon_pick()` closes it without a callback.
- `add_kill_feed(killer: String, victim: String, weapon_name: String, weapon_icon: String, headshot: bool, mine: bool, verb := "")`
  (verb replaces ELIMINATED / HEADSHOT: "LEFT" with killer = the player who left, victim "")
- `kill_banner(victim_name: String, headshot: bool)` (big elimination feedback when the player kills),
  `out_banner(victim_name, cause)` (the opponent went down by itself: cause "zone", "fall" or "self"
  in `HUD.OUT_LINES`; no badge, no red X, a line in its place), `banner_kind()` ("", "kill" or the cause),
  `confirm_kill(pos: Vector3)` (the kill's red X, sound and buzz; the arena calls it for an online
  kill, which the other phone reports as its death)
- the target dummy (`arena.practice`): the weapon panel's middle slot reads CHANGE GUN and calls
  `arena.repick()`, which opens the weapon pick during the fight
- `reveal(f: Fighter, seconds)` (enemy shows on the minimap), `match_over: bool`, `tips_time: float`
- `editor_mode`, `selected_id`, `layout` (used by the HUD editor)
- read by the screenshot tours: `panels` (HudPanels: weapon pick + scoreboard), `seen`
  ({Fighter: bool} line of sight, refreshed 10x per second)
- helpers live in their own files: `hud_draw.gd` (HudDraw: icons, buttons, text, shapes shared
  with the editor), `hud_minimap.gd` (HudMinimap), `hud_panels.gd` (HudPanels)
- online matches: `bind` adds `HudNet` (scripts/hud_net.gd, node "HudNet", its own layer): the
  round trip to the other phone under the gear (NetPing), CHAT / MUTE (NetChat) and TALK / SOUND
  (NetVoice) right of the minimap, the chat feed under the score bar (`feed`, `add_line`, `send`),
  the chat panel `ChatPanel` (`open_panel`, `close_panel`; the HUD's input is off while it is open).
  Its buttons take their touches before the HUD (a child's _input runs first). Every other match
  (TRAINING GROUND) gets it too with `net` false (`HudNet.attach(hud, false)`): only the ping chip,
  the relay's round trip while connected (`Cloud.relay_ping()`), else a grey "--", no tag.

## HUD layout (scripts/hud_layout.gd, version 5)
- A movable widget is a Dictionary {x, y, s, o, a}: `x` centre in widths of the 20:9 reference
  screen, `y` centre and `s` size in screen heights, `o` opacity 0.1..1, `a` anchor "l" | "c" |
  "r" (keeps the distance to that edge, in screen heights, from 16:9 to 22:9). Defaults
  (`HudLayout.DEFAULTS`) are the reference phone's places, except PRONE (under the weapon panel,
  left of SCOPE: the reference's place beside the score bar is under the panel below 18:9); no
  two default touch areas overlap from 16:10 to 22:9 but the gear's on the panel's corner.
  RELOAD (`reload`, up and left of FIRE; grey on a full magazine, without reserve or with the fists
  out) and SWITCH (`swap`, right beside RELOAD on its left, the reference's place: gun <-> fists,
  showing the other one; a layout saved with it at its old place under the panel takes the new).
  `HUD.button_enabled(id)`: the settings left_fire (2 = OFF), reload_button, switch_button and
  freelook_button turn those buttons off in a match; the editor still places them (label "OFF").
- `HudLayout.resolve(overrides)` -> full clamped layout (unknown ids and wrongly typed values
  dropped; a widget saved exactly at an old default place in `MOVED` takes the new one),
  `current()` (from `Game.hud_layout`, ignored unless its `_v` is `HudLayout.VERSION`),
  `to_save(layout)` -> what `Game.save_hud_layout()` stores.
- Pixels: `center(w, vs)`, `place(w, pos, vs)` (picks the anchor from the screen third),
  `hit_rect(id, w, vs)`, `touch_radius(w, vs)` (round buttons, at least `MIN_TOUCH` = 0.036 h;
  a tap counts to 1.15 x it), `minimap_rect()`, `weapon_main()` / `weapon_slots()` (weapon panel).
  `HudLayout.inset` (canvas px, x left, y right): a camera cutout's sides, which the HUD keeps up
  to date from `UI.safe_margins(viewport)`; edge-anchored widgets move in by it.
- The HUD draws the weapon panel, then the round buttons, then the gear, and a tap goes to what is
  drawn on top (a button the player put over the panel takes the taps there); the editor
  (scripts/hud_editor.gd) picks widgets the same way.

## Player (scripts/player.gd) -> called by the HUD's touch controls
- movement `move_dir: Vector2` (joystick, length 0..1, y forward), `set_sprint(on)`
- `add_look(rel_px: Vector2, from_fire_button: bool)`, `set_firing(on)`, `toggle_ads()`,
  `do_jump()`, `toggle_crouch()`, `toggle_prone()`, `do_reload()`, `select_weapon(i)` (0 gun, 1 fists),
  `use_medkit()`, `use_shield()`, `use_grenade()`
- state: `hp` (0..Fighter.MAX_HP=200), `alive`, `weapons[i]` (WeaponState: `def`, `ammo`,
  `reserve`, `reloading`, `reload_progress()`, `infinite()`), `current`, `stance`
  (Fighter.Stance.STAND/CROUCH/PRONE), `sprinting`, `ads`, `is_scoped()`, `scope_type()`
  ("reddot" | "sniper" | "none"), `healing`, `heal_progress()`, `medkits`, `shields`,
  `grenades`, `crosshair_on_target` (crosshair turns red), `current_spread()` (deg),
  `camera`, `firing`, `assist_target`, `hold_fire` (prep: cannot shoot)
- camera: over the right shoulder (`Player.CAM_SHOULDER` right of the head and `CAM_RAISE` up, in
  the view's axes, `CAM_DIST` behind); a sphere of `CAM_RADIUS` swept from the head to that pivot
  and by the spring arm behind it, against `CAM_MASK` (the map and deployed walls), keeps it out
  of walls; the own model is hidden with the camera within `HEAD_CLEAR` of the head (and in the
  scope) and see-through within `FADE_DIST` or while `covers_crosshair()` (a hit zone or the gun
  within `COVER_GAP` of the crosshair's ray: it would hide what the crosshair is on)
- signals: `hit_landed(target, amount, headshot, pos, killed)`, `damaged(amount, from_pos, headshot, attacker)`;
  a shot reports `hit_landed` once for its head part (headshot true) and once for its body part,
  each the HP the target really lost, `killed` with the last (`Fighter.land_hits(hits)` lands
  [target, zone, damage before armour, point] hits without a scene: the unit tests)
- `Fighter.take_damage(amount, zone, attacker, from_pos, cause := "")` returns the HP it really took:
  `hit_points(amount, zone)` (after `armor_mult(zone)`, whole points but for the zone and falls),
  never more than was left; a `RemoteFighter` returns what the hit will take on the other phone as
  this phone's hits leave its HP (`hp_left`). Cause "frag" for a frag
  (kept as `last_hit_cause`; the kill feed then reads FRAG). `Player.drawn_position()`: where the
  body is drawn this frame (between the last two physics ticks at 90 / 120 FPS; the camera and the
  own model follow it).
- `Weapons.HELMET_REDUCTION` / `VEST_REDUCTION` (Lv2 armour; show level 2 icons)

## Weapons (scripts/weapons.gd)
- `Weapons.DEFS[id]` with `name`, `class` (AR/SMG/SG/PISTOL/SNIPER/MELEE), `icon` (texture name in
  res://assets/ui/icons/, e.g. "weapon_ar"; missing icons fall back to the AR), `mag`, `reserve`,
  `crosshair` ("ring" | "wide" | "dot"), `scope`, `auto`. `Weapons.PICKABLE` = pick-grid order.
  Movement by class: `Weapons.CLASS_MOVE` / `move_mult(def)` x `Fighter.RUN_SPEED` etc. (the rifle's
  speeds): fists 1.15, SMG 1.06, rifle and pistol 1.0, shotgun 0.97, marksman 0.95, sniper 0.92.

## Map (scripts/map_builder.gd) -> used by arena.gd
- `build(parent: Node3D)` creates the environment (`env: WorldEnvironment`, `sun: DirectionalLight3D`),
  geometry, colliders (layer Fighter.LAYER_WORLD), the navigation region `nav` (baked by arena),
  `obstacles` (Array[Rect2] X/Z footprints for the minimap/AI), `cover_points` (Array[Vector3])
- `spawn_a`, `spawn_a_yaw` (player), `spawn_b`, `spawn_b_yaw` (bot); `map_name`; `map_rect`;
  `zone_center`; `zone_start_radius`
- `set_barriers(on: bool)` red spawn barriers (colliders + glow) during PREP
- `apply_quality(g: int)` 0 LOW .. 3 EXTREME
- Visual layers: the top-down minimap camera sees only layer 1 (floor, containers, crates, the
  white wall caps that outline the walls). Roof, ceiling, trusses and hanging lamps are on 2
  (`LAYER_ROOF`); characters, weapons, effects, the zone wall and the spawn barriers on 4
  (`LAYER_FX` = `CharacterModel.LAYER_ACTORS`); corrugated walls, columns and lintels on 16
  (`LAYER_WALLS`, not lit by the sun or the fill light).

## Menus
- Main scene: `scenes/loading.tscn` (`Loading`, scripts/loading.gd). The boot screen preloads
  the character, animation and prop GLBs, the sounds and the lobby on worker threads, keeps
  them referenced by the root node `LoadingCache` (meta `resources`) for the app's lifetime,
  then calls `Game.goto_login()` (after `TIMEOUT_S` at the latest; loads still running then are
  handed to the `LoadingCache` node, a `Loading.Cache`, which collects them as they finish).
  Automated runs skip it; `Loading.hold_for_test = true` keeps it up for a screenshot tour.
  Once the loads are in, a `ShaderWarmup` (scripts/shader_warmup.gd, under the root) draws the
  arena and a fighter off-screen for a few frames so their shaders compile there, not in a
  match's first frames (`ShaderWarmup.kept` holds the materials; not in automated or headless runs).
- `IntroSting` (scripts/intro_sting.gd): the launch sting, `IntroSting.play_over(host)` puts it
  over the boot screen in its own CanvasLayer `IntroLayer` (layer 90) while the boot loads run;
  the boot screen leaves only once `IntroSting.playing(host)` is false. 2.4 s (`LENGTH`), synced
  to intro_sting.wav (impact `HIT` 0.90 s); a tap or back after 0.4 s skips it; at the end the
  lockup lands on the boot screen's logo. Automated runs skip it unless
  `IntroSting.show_for_test`; `hold_at(t)` freezes it for screenshots.
- `Loading.start_match(host)`: match-loading overlay (CanvasLayer 100, mode + map card
  `MatchCard`: `MatchMode`, `MatchChips`; the texts come from the static
  `Loading.match_info(online, opponent, dummy, difficulty, rounds)`: TRAINING GROUND "FIRST TO
  n / 1 VS BOT / BOT: <level>" or "TARGET DUMMY", LONE WOLF "FIRST TO n / 1 VS 1 ONLINE / VS
  <name>") that loads the arena on a thread, then `Game.goto_arena()`. Used by START, an online
  challenge and PLAY AGAIN.
- `ResultsScreen.new()` (Control): `setup(result: Dictionary)` - keys: won, draw, invalid, kills,
  damage, headshots, accuracy, time, xp, enemy, mode (the name shown), mode_id ("training" |
  "lonewolf", the profile counts the match there), map, weapon, score_player, score_bot,
  hp_left, difficulty (the bot's level, or "ONLINE"), taken, opponent_left (the other player left: the
  VICTORY screen says so, `OpponentLeft`, their row reads LEFT); shots, hits, deaths and enemy_id (the
  online opponent's player id, else "") go to the profile only (`Game.record_match`). Hides the arena's `hud` while
  shown; the opponent's orange row
  (`EnemyRow`) comes from the current scene's `scoreboard_rows()`. Buttons: PLAY AGAIN (match
  overlay, then `Game.goto_arena`), LOBBY (`Game.goto_lobby`); Android back = LOBBY (ignored while
  PLAY AGAIN loads). `ResultsScreen.honour(result)` (static) picks the honour tag. An internet LONE
  WOLF match (enemy_id, not local) has `Rematch` in PLAY AGAIN's place, `AddFriend` (not friends
  yet: `Cloud.request_friend`) and the line `RematchStatus`: REMATCH while the session is up sends
  "rm" [takes their challenges] (`Arena.ask_rematch`; the other screen says "<NAME> WANTS A
  REMATCH", `Arena.rematch_asked` / `rematch_wanted` / `rematch_they_take`); once both want it both
  call `Arena.leave_session()` (the arena then leaves the new session alone and Game.online to it)
  and a fresh `Cloud.invite` goes from the phone whose challenge the other takes (the old host when
  both do, `Arena.hosted()`), which the other screen takes (`Cloud.invited` there: the old
  opponent's challenge is the rematch, anyone else's is declined); `session_started` loads the
  match. With the session gone a tap sends the fresh challenge itself. Off after a forfeit.
- `PauseMenu.new()` (Control, works while paused): the in-match menu behind the HUD's gear and
  Android back, signal `closed`. It is the settings at once (`settings`, a `SettingsPanel` with
  `in_match = true`, added at the end of the frame it opens in) with `Quit` (QUIT MATCH, asks once
  more: its second tap may come up to `PauseMenu.QUIT_CONFIRM_S` real seconds later) at the foot of
  their tab column, and `edit_hud` set: EDIT CUSTOM HUD opens `HudEditor` (scripts/
  hud_editor_overlay.gd: the lobby's editor, BACK or back closes it to the settings; SAVE reloads
  the match HUD's layout) over the match. The settings' X or back closes the menu (saved; also
  when the arena closes it). Hides `arena.hud` while open (restored on close and exit; a hidden HUD
  ignores touches). It ignores a back request in the frame it opens (the engine hands the request
  that opened it to it as well). `Arena.open_pause()` opens one at most and pauses a TRAINING
  GROUND match while it is open; online the tree is not paused (an open menu is what blocks a
  second one): the match runs on, and the end of a round or of the match closes it
  (`Arena.close_pause()`, also before the results).
- `SettingsPanel.new()` (Control): signal `closed`; writes `Game.settings` keys listed in
  `autoload/game.gd` DEFAULT_SETTINGS and calls `Game.save_settings()` on close; a graphics or

## DUO / SQUAD: between two phones (WP-N: team_arena_net, Cloud team invites and seeks, RemoteFighter)
- Cloud (additive; the 1 vs 1 paths are unchanged):
  - `team_invite(peer_id, size, coop, quick := false) -> bool`: like `invite()` (needs the peer
    listed, nothing going out, connected; this phone hosts). Inbox JSON {"t": "tinv", "from",
    "name", "bundle", "sec", "pk", "sig"}: the same fields as a duel challenge. The RSA part is the
    80 key bytes plus one team byte (`Cloud.team_byte`: size | coop << 3 | quick << 4;
    `team_from_byte` reads it back, {} for anything else), so the relay never sees the size.
    `sig` signs `Cloud.team_invite_text(from, to, sec)` ("tinv|..."): required, and a duel's
    signature does not pass for it (nor the other way round). acc / dec / can are the duel's.
  - Receiving one (`_on_team_invite`): unsigned, blocked, or no team byte: ignored. Busy: "dec" at
    once. quick: taken at once only while `seeking`, `seek_team == size`, the sender in
    `quick_seen` and `NetMatch.allowed`; else "dec". coop: only from a friend that DUEL
    CHALLENGES FROM lets through (`NetMatch.allowed`) and while `invited` has a listener (the
    lobby); else "dec". Shown through `invited` like a duel; `invite_from()` now also returns
    "team": {} for a duel, {"size", "coop"} for a TEAM INVITE.
  - `session_team`: {"size", "coop"} on both phones of a session a "tinv" started (set when it
    goes out / is accepted, so it is there on `session_started`); {} for a duel; cleared by
    `cancel_invite` (before the session), `invite()` and `end_session`.
  - `team_seek_start(size)` / `team_seek_stop()`: the search on ROOT/mm/t<size> (`seek_team`);
    `send_seek(saw)` publishes on the running search's topic and only that topic's seeks are heard
    (`seeker_seen`, `quick_seen`). `seek_start()` (1 vs 1) leaves a team search first; a 1 vs 1
    quick challenge is never taken during a team search. `quick_seen` keeps at most
    `Cloud.QUICK_SEEN_MAX` (64) seekers: the one heard longest ago makes room.
- Privacy Notice (Login.privacy_sections): names TEAM INVITES (size encrypted) and the random-match
  searches (player ID and the seen IDs); tests/team_net_unit.gd checks the wording.
