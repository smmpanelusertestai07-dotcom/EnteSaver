# Interfaces between the game's parts (keep these stable)

Owners: `arena.gd`, `fighter.gd`, `player.gd`, `bot.gd`, `weapons.gd`, `character_model.gd` are the
gameplay core. HUD, map and menus are separate files that talk to the core only through what is
listed here. If a part needs something new from the core, add it in its own file or note it.

## Arena (scripts/arena.gd) -> read by HUD / menus
- `phase` (Phase.PREP=0, FIGHT=1, END=2 (match over), BREAK=3 (between rounds)), `prep_left` (s),
  `time_left` (s), `elapsed` (s, this round), `match_time` (s), `round_index`, `rounds_to_win`
  (`Game.rounds_to_win(online)`: 5, a custom room's own 1-7 online, LW_ROUNDS_TO_WIN in test
  builds), `score` {"player": int, "bot": int}, `picked_weapon` (id),
  `pick_note` (prep line: who picks the gun, or which it is)
- the match's mode: `mode` ("training" | "lonewolf", a key of `Game.MODE_NAMES`), `mode_name`
  (what the HUD, scoreboard, pause menu and results show: TRAINING GROUND against the bot or the
  target dummy, LONE WOLF online), `practice` (the target dummy: endless HP, no zone, the clock
  counts up, nothing recorded), `online` (the opponent is another phone)
- zone: `zone_state` ("stationary" | "shrinking" | "closed"), `zone_next()` (s until it
  changes), `zone_radius`, `zone_center` (Vector3), `in_zone(pos)`, `zone_warning` (text or "")
- `fighters` (Array[Fighter]), `player`, `bot`, `map_name` ("CARGO CAGE"), `map_rect` (Rect2,
  world X/Z area of the minimap), `minimap_texture` (Texture2D rendered once from above, may be
  null for the first frames), `obstacles` (Array[Rect2] footprints, X/Z)
- `scoreboard_rows()` -> [{name, me, alive, hp, kills, damage, headshots, accuracy, rounds, weapon}]
- `open_pause()`; the HUD emits `pause_pressed` for it. `record_left()`: the pause menu's QUIT
  MATCH puts a counted match into the profile as LEFT, a defeat (HISTORY result "left"), with what
  was played so far; a match already decided keeps its own result.
- An online session that ends before the match (Cloud `session_ended(reason)`): "left" (the other
  phone said bye or went silent) is a forfeit win once the first fight began; "connection lost"
  (this phone lost the relay, or was away about as long as the other waits: `Cloud.AWAY_MS`) is a
  LEFT as with QUIT MATCH. Either way a match whose first fight had not begun counts nowhere; those
  go back to the lobby, which shows `Game.match_notice` ([title, text]) once. What the other
  phone's events may make here is capped (`spawn_cap(kind)`: its walls and frags per round).

## Arena -> HUD calls (scripts/hud.gd must implement all of these)
- `bind(player: Player, arena: Node)`, `reload_layout()`, `release_all()`
- `show_center(text, seconds, color := Color.WHITE, size := 96, sub := "")`
- `show_round_banner(round_index: int, area: String)`  (blue->orange band "ROUND 1 / AREA: ...")
- `open_weapon_pick(options: Array[String] weapon ids, preselect: String, seconds: float, on_pick: Callable(id))`
  must call `on_pick` exactly once (tap SELECT, or the timer runs out -> current choice);
  `close_weapon_pick()` closes it without a callback.
- `add_kill_feed(killer: String, victim: String, weapon_name: String, weapon_icon: String, headshot: bool, mine: bool)`
- `kill_banner(victim_name: String, headshot: bool)` (big elimination feedback when the player kills),
  `confirm_kill(pos: Vector3)` (the kill's red X, sound and buzz; the arena calls it for an online
  kill, which the other phone reports as its death)
- the target dummy (`arena.practice`): the weapon panel's middle slot reads CHANGE GUN and calls
  `arena.repick()`, which opens the weapon pick during the fight
- `reveal(f: Fighter, seconds)` (enemy shows on the minimap), `match_over: bool`, `tips_time: float`
- `editor_mode`, `selected_id`, `layout` (used by the HUD editor)
- read by the screenshot tours: `panels` (HudPanels: weapon pick + scoreboard), `seen`
  ({Fighter: bool} line of sight, refreshed 10x per second)
- helpers live in their own files: `hud_draw.gd` (HudDraw: icons, buttons, text, shapes shared
  with the editor), `hud_minimap.gd` (HudMinimap), `hud_panels.gd` (HudPanels)

## HUD layout (scripts/hud_layout.gd, version 3)
- A movable widget is a Dictionary {x, y, s, o, a}: `x` centre in widths of the 20:9 reference
  screen, `y` centre and `s` size in screen heights, `o` opacity 0.1..1, `a` anchor "l" | "c" |
  "r" (keeps the distance to that edge, in screen heights, from 16:9 to 22:9). Defaults
  (`HudLayout.DEFAULTS`) are the reference phone's places, except PRONE (under the weapon panel,
  left of SCOPE: the reference's place beside the score bar is under the panel below 18:9); no
  two default touch areas overlap from 16:10 to 22:9 but the gear's on the panel's corner.
- `HudLayout.resolve(overrides)` -> full clamped layout (unknown ids and wrongly typed values
  dropped; a widget saved exactly at an old default place in `MOVED` takes the new one),
  `current()` (from `Game.hud_layout`, ignored unless its `_v` is `HudLayout.VERSION`),
  `to_save(layout)` -> what `Game.save_hud_layout()` stores.
- Pixels: `center(w, vs)`, `place(w, pos, vs)` (picks the anchor from the screen third),
  `hit_rect(id, w, vs)`, `touch_radius(w, vs)` (round buttons, at least `MIN_TOUCH` = 0.036 h;
  a tap counts to 1.15 x it), `minimap_rect()`, `weapon_main()` / `weapon_slots()` (weapon panel).
  `HudLayout.inset` (canvas px, x left, y right): a camera cutout's sides, which the HUD keeps up
  to date from `UI.safe_margins(viewport)`; edge-anchored widgets move in by it.
- The HUD draws the weapon panel, then the round buttons, then the gear, and a tap goes to what is
  drawn on top (a button the player put over the panel takes the taps there); the editor
  (scripts/hud_editor.gd) picks widgets the same way.

## Android log lines (read by tools/ci/emulator_test.sh)
- `[LoneWolf] boot screen`, `intro sting`, `intro done`, `boot loaded <n> files in <s>s` (or
  `boot timeout` and `boot timeout: <n> files still loading`), `login ready`, `profile setup`,
  `profile created` (the first-run guest login), `lobby ready`,
  `prep started`, `weapon picked: <id>`, `match started`, `player fired`,
  `player moved`, `pause opened`, `pause closed`, `<player|bot> eliminated`, `time up`,
  `round over`, `round <n>`, `match over: <won|lost|draw>`, `results shown`,
  `online match: <host|guest>` (a LONE WOLF online 1 vs 1, see scripts/cloud.gd: the game's
  only transport; there is no local-network play),
  `signature <state>` (Guard, "ok" when the package manager confirms a signer listed in the
  build's res://build_keys.gd).
- `Game.mark_ui(name, item, local_point)` logs `[LoneWolf] ui <name> <x> <y>` in screen pixels
  (guest, name_field, confirm, start, fire, joystick) so the test taps controls where the current
  layout puts them.

## Player (scripts/player.gd) -> called by the HUD's touch controls
- movement `move_dir: Vector2` (joystick, length 0..1, y forward), `set_sprint(on)`
- `add_look(rel_px: Vector2, from_fire_button: bool)`, `set_firing(on)`, `toggle_ads()`,
  `do_jump()`, `toggle_crouch()`, `toggle_prone()`, `do_reload()`, `select_weapon(i)` (0 gun, 1 fists),
  `use_medkit()`, `use_shield()`, `use_grenade()`
- state: `hp` (0..Fighter.MAX_HP=200), `alive`, `weapons[i]` (WeaponState: `def`, `ammo`,
  `reserve`, `reloading`, `reload_progress()`, `infinite()`), `current`, `stance`
  (Fighter.Stance.STAND/CROUCH/PRONE), `sprinting`, `ads`, `is_scoped()`, `scope_type()`
  ("reddot" | "sniper" | "none"), `healing`, `heal_progress()`, `medkits`, `shields`,
  `grenades`, `crosshair_on_target` (crosshair turns red), `current_spread()` (deg),
  `camera`, `firing`, `assist_target`, `hold_fire` (prep: cannot shoot)
- signals: `hit_landed(target, amount, headshot, pos, killed)`, `damaged(amount, from_pos, headshot, attacker)`
- `Fighter.take_damage(amount, zone, attacker, from_pos, cause := "")`: cause "frag" for a frag
  (kept as `last_hit_cause`; the kill feed then reads FRAG). `Player.drawn_position()`: where the
  body is drawn this frame (between the last two physics ticks at 90 / 120 FPS; the camera and the
  own model follow it).
- `Weapons.HELMET_REDUCTION` / `VEST_REDUCTION` (Lv2 armour; show level 2 icons)

## Weapons (scripts/weapons.gd)
- `Weapons.DEFS[id]` with `name`, `class` (AR/SMG/SG/PISTOL/SNIPER/MELEE), `icon` (texture name in
  res://assets/ui/icons/, e.g. "weapon_ar"; missing icons fall back to the AR), `mag`, `reserve`,
  `crosshair` ("ring" | "wide" | "dot"), `scope`, `auto`. `Weapons.PICKABLE` = pick-grid order.

## Map (scripts/map_builder.gd) -> used by arena.gd
- `build(parent: Node3D)` creates the environment (`env: WorldEnvironment`, `sun: DirectionalLight3D`),
  geometry, colliders (layer Fighter.LAYER_WORLD), the navigation region `nav` (baked by arena),
  `obstacles` (Array[Rect2] X/Z footprints for the minimap/AI), `cover_points` (Array[Vector3])
- `spawn_a`, `spawn_a_yaw` (player), `spawn_b`, `spawn_b_yaw` (bot); `map_name`; `map_rect`;
  `zone_center`; `zone_start_radius`
- `set_barriers(on: bool)` red spawn barriers (colliders + glow) during PREP
- `apply_quality(g: int)` 0 LOW .. 3 EXTREME
- Visual layers: the top-down minimap camera sees only layer 1 (floor, containers, crates, the
  white wall caps that outline the walls). Roof, ceiling, trusses and hanging lamps are on 2
  (`LAYER_ROOF`); characters, weapons, effects, the zone wall and the spawn barriers on 4
  (`LAYER_FX` = `CharacterModel.LAYER_ACTORS`); corrugated walls, columns and lintels on 16
  (`LAYER_WALLS`, not lit by the sun or the fill light).

## Menus
- Main scene: `scenes/loading.tscn` (`Loading`, scripts/loading.gd). The boot screen preloads
  the character, animation and prop GLBs, the sounds and the lobby on worker threads, keeps
  them referenced by the root node `LoadingCache` (meta `resources`) for the app's lifetime,
  then calls `Game.goto_login()` (after `TIMEOUT_S` at the latest; loads still running then are
  handed to the `LoadingCache` node, a `Loading.Cache`, which collects them as they finish).
  Automated runs skip it; `Loading.hold_for_test = true` keeps it up for a screenshot tour.
- `IntroSting` (scripts/intro_sting.gd): the launch sting, `IntroSting.play_over(host)` puts it
  over the boot screen in its own CanvasLayer `IntroLayer` (layer 90) while the boot loads run;
  the boot screen leaves only once `IntroSting.playing(host)` is false. 2.4 s (`LENGTH`), synced
  to intro_sting.wav (impact `HIT` 0.90 s); a tap or back after 0.4 s skips it; at the end the
  lockup lands on the boot screen's logo. Automated runs skip it unless
  `IntroSting.show_for_test`; `hold_at(t)` freezes it for screenshots.
- `Loading.start_match(host)`: match-loading overlay (CanvasLayer 100, mode + map card
  `MatchCard`: `MatchMode`, `MatchChips`; the texts come from the static
  `Loading.match_info(online, opponent, dummy, difficulty, rounds)`: TRAINING GROUND "FIRST TO
  n / 1 VS BOT / BOT: <level>" or "TARGET DUMMY", LONE WOLF "FIRST TO n / 1 VS 1 ONLINE / VS
  <name>") that loads the arena on a thread, then `Game.goto_arena()`. Used by START, an online
  challenge and PLAY AGAIN.
- `ResultsScreen.new()` (Control): `setup(result: Dictionary)` - keys: won, draw, invalid, kills,
  damage, headshots, accuracy, time, xp, enemy, mode (the name shown), mode_id ("training" |
  "lonewolf", the profile counts the match there), map, weapon, score_player, score_bot,
  hp_left, difficulty (the bot's level, or "ONLINE"), taken; shots, hits, deaths and enemy_id (the
  online opponent's player id, else "") go to the profile only (`Game.record_match`). Hides the arena's `hud` while
  shown; the opponent's orange row
  (`EnemyRow`) comes from the current scene's `scoreboard_rows()`. Buttons: PLAY AGAIN (match
  overlay, then `Game.goto_arena`), LOBBY (`Game.goto_lobby`); Android back = LOBBY (ignored while
  PLAY AGAIN loads). `ResultsScreen.honour(result)` (static) picks the honour tag.
- `PauseMenu.new()` (Control, works while paused): signal `closed`. Hides `arena.hud` while open
  (restored on close and exit). It ignores a back request in the frame it opens (the engine hands
  the request that opened it to it as well); a second back closes it. Opens `SettingsPanel` with
  `in_match = true`.
- `SettingsPanel.new()` (Control): signal `closed`; writes `Game.settings` keys listed in
  `autoload/game.gd` DEFAULT_SETTINGS and calls `Game.save_settings()` on close; a graphics or
  visual-effects change emits `Game.settings_changed` so the arena and the lobby re-apply them at
  once. `TAB_KEYS` lists each tab's settings (its RESET), `NOT_SHOWN` the rest; RESET and EXTREME
  ask first in a `Confirm` layer (`ConfirmOk`, `ConfirmCancel`; back closes it). With
  `in_match` (also detected when the current scene has `open_pause()`), EDIT CUSTOM HUD becomes a
  note (`EditHudNote`): leaving for the editor would abandon the match.
- Camera cutout: `UI.safe_margins(viewport)` is the screen's left and right sides a cutout covers
  (canvas px; zero on desktop builds; `UI.margins_for_test` stands in for one in checks);
  `UI.keep_clear_of_cutout(control)` keeps a full-screen Control's content clear of it (the
  lobby's main UI, the pause band's content; the results screen and the HUD do the same).
- Performance: `PerfGovernor.attach(scene, level_scale, dynamic := true)` (scripts/perf_governor.gd)
  from `Arena.apply_graphics()` (dynamic 3D resolution, 0.6 at the least) and the lobby's
  `_apply_quality()` (measuring only). AUTO graphics: `Game.start_auto_graphics()` (a first guess
  from `PerfGovernor.device_level()`, then `Game.auto_graphics_pending` until a scene's governor
  measured the frames and called `Game.finish_auto_graphics(level)`), `Game.stop_auto_graphics()`.
  Frame rates offered: `Game.fps_choices()`. Effects: `Effects.configure(level, visual_effects)`;
  the bullet holes are one MultiMesh per scene (`Effects.MAX_HOLES`, `hole_count()`). The arena
  sets the graphics up again only when the graphics level or visual effects change (another save,
  such as the gun pick's, keeps the governor's state).
- Aim settings on `Player`: `sight_of(def)` ("sniper", "2x", "reddot", "none"), `sens_key(def,
  aiming)` (the sensitivity setting a view turns with), `assist_allowed(aim_precision, aiming)`.
- Lobby Android back closes the top layer (popup: `dialogs.back()`, the popup's own way back;
  settings, PROFILE, wardrobe), then shows a quit dialog (`QuitDialog`, `QuitCancel`,
  `QuitConfirm`); it is ignored during the START overlay.
- The lobby (`Lobby`, scripts/lobby.gd: stage, top bar, menus, mode card, START, settings,
  input) makes four helpers that build under its `ui` and reach it through their `lobby`:
  `dialogs` (`LobbyDialogs`: `open_modal(title, width, top)` / `close_modal()` / `modal`, the one
  open popup, `back()` (its X and Android back: the popup's meta "on_back" Callable, else close),
  `confirm(title, text, ok_text, on_ok, on_cancel, danger)` (`ConfirmOk`, `ConfirmCancel`),
  `notice(title, text)` (one OK: `NoticeText`, `NoticeOk`); SELECT
  MODE `select_mode()`, HOW TO PLAY, the name editor, the quit dialog), `friends`
  (`LobbyFriends`: the ONLINE 1 VS 1 window `open(kind, back)` with its list in `NetScroll`
  (back: reopened under a closing player card, the list scrolls back to where it was), custom
  rooms, the online service's signals; `challenge(id)`, `add_friend(id)`), `wardrobe_ui`
  (`LobbyWardrobe`: WARDROBE, every outfit free; `LobbyWardrobe.outfit_color(id)`) and
  `profile_ui` (`LobbyProfile`, below). Their scrolling lists (PROFILE's tabs, `NetScroll`) go
  through `UI.drag_scroll(scroll, content)` each time their rows are built: a finger drag that
  starts on a row scrolls them (rows and their buttons pass the touch on; a tap still presses).
  Tours read `model`, `ui`, `start_btn`, `mode_card`,
  `_modal`, `wardrobe`, `_preview_bundle` and `profile_ui` from the lobby and call its
  `_open_wardrobe()`, `_close_wardrobe()`, `_open_profile()` and `_open_settings()`;
  `refresh_player_card()` redraws the player card top-left (name, profile picture).
- PROFILE (`LobbyProfile`, scripts/lobby_profile.gd): `PlayerCard` opens it (`open(tab)`,
  `close()`, `layer` = the Control `Profile`; `ProfileClose` or back closes it). Tabs
  `ProfileTab_<career|history|namecard|blocked>`: CAREER (`career_stats(profile)`: rates and
  averages "-" without data; `Career_<key>`, `CareerMode_<mode>`), HISTORY (`HistoryRow_<n>`,
  `HistoryEmpty`), NAME CARD (`Banner_<id>`, `Avatar_<id>`, saved at once), BLOCKED
  (`BlockedRow_<code>`: `LobbyProfile.who_label(id)`, the name kept with the block else `#CODE`;
  `Unblock_<code>` asks first, `BlockedEmpty`; it follows blocks made elsewhere, and while it or a
  card opened from PROFILE shows, `Cloud.start_directory("profile")` gets the player cards). The
  name card (`NameCard`): `CardAvatar`,
  `CardName` + `CardEditName`, `CardLevel` + `CardXp`, `CardId` + `CardCopy` (the whole id to the
  clipboard), `CardSignature` + `CardEditSignature` (`SignatureEdit`, `SignatureOk`), `CardOutfit`,
  `CardSince`.
- The player card: `profile_ui.open_player(id, back_to)` (a `Modal`, `PlayerCardPopup`), opened by
  `Player_<code>` (`LobbyProfile.player_button`) in the ONLINE window's lists and in BLOCKED: their
  `Cloud.directory` card, `PlayerStatus` (online now / last seen), `PlayerId` + `PlayerCopy`,
  `PlayerDuel` (online only, `LobbyFriends.challenge`), `PlayerFriend` (ADD FRIEND / REQUEST SENT /
  REMOVE FRIEND), `PlayerBlock` (BLOCK / UNBLOCK: `Game.block` / `Game.unblock`); both ask first.
  X and back reopen the ONLINE tab `back_to` ("" just closes). BLOCK first calls
  `Cloud.drop_player(id)`: the challenge sent to them is called off (they could still accept it),
  theirs is dropped, they lose their seat in the hosted room and the friend request sent to them
  is taken back (`withdraw_request(id, notify)`: offline its deletion on the relay waits in the
  profile's "withdrawn" until the next connection); Cloud ignores a blocked player's challenge,
  acceptance, friend request, room request and room "join".
- Modes (both 1 vs 1 on CARGO CAGE): `Game.mode` is "training" (TRAINING GROUND, vs a bot or the
  target dummy, the default) or "lonewolf" (LONE WOLF, online 1 vs 1), kept as the setting
  "mode"; `Game.MODE_NAMES`, `Game.mode_name(m := "")`, `Game.ROUNDS_TO_WIN` (5) and
  `Game.rounds_to_win(online_match)`. The lobby's mode card (`ModeCard`: `ModeName`,
  `ModeFormat`) opens SELECT MODE (a `Modal` with the cards `Mode_training` / `Mode_lonewolf`);
  the opponent chips `Diff_EASY|NORMAL|HARD|DUMMY` (row `Difficulty`) show in TRAINING GROUND
  only, DUMMY sets `Game.practice` (session only, an online match ignores it). START: TRAINING
  GROUND -> `Loading.start_match` (caption LOADING); LONE WOLF -> `friends.open("online")`.
  `Game.count_match(mode)` and `Game.record_match(result)` count the totals and
  `profile.modes[mode]` (matches, wins, kills); the target dummy counts nowhere.
- Saves (profile.json, settings.json, hud_layout.json) go through `Game.write_json(path, data)`:
  a temporary file, checked whole, then renamed over the old one, which stays as `<path>.bak`;
  `Game.read_json(path)` falls back to it when the file does not read (a write cut short on a
  full phone). Their keys: the settings keys are `Game.DEFAULT_SETTINGS` (`SETTING_RANGES`, `BOOL_SETTINGS`; older
  files are migrated by settings_version), the profile keys `Game.DEFAULT_PROFILE` (signed as a
  whole; older profiles are migrated by profile_version). Crosshair: `Game.crosshair_color()`,
  `Game.crosshair_scale()`. Blocks: profile "blocked" = [{id, name ("" when unknown), t}], oldest
  first, `Game.MAX_BLOCKED` = 50 (`Game.block(id, name)` lifts the oldest when full:
  `Game.block_lifts()`; `Game.unblock(id)`, `Game.is_blocked(id)`, `Game.blocked_ids()`; a bare id
  saved by an earlier build loads as a block without a name). "withdrawn": ids whose friend request
  was taken back offline (Cloud deletes it on the relay at the next connection). Profile version 3
  adds "history" (`Game.MAX_HISTORY` = 20 entries,
  newest first: {result won|lost|draw, mode, enemy, id, score, enemy_score, kills, damage,
  headshots, t}; result "left": quit with QUIT MATCH), added by `Game.record_match` and checked
  entry by entry on load; a row with an opponent's id opens their card; a version 2
  profile starts it empty.
- Public player card v2 (cloud.gd, retained on ROOT/p/<id>): {"t": "card", "v": 2, id, name,
  bundle, avatar, banner, level, signature, pk, ts, sig, sig2}; sig2 signs
  "card2|id|name|bundle|avatar|banner|level|signature|ts" (`Cloud.card2_text`), sig the v1 text
  "card|id|name|bundle|ts" (older builds list it; cards without "v" are v1 and still accepted).
  `Cloud.my_card()` is everything public; the cards are listened to while anyone wants them
  (`Cloud.start_directory(by)` / `stop_directory(by)`: the ONLINE window "window", PROFILE
  "profile"); `_check_card` checks every field (types, size, no "|",
  avatar / banner known else the default, level 1..CARD_LEVEL_MAX, `Game.clean_text` signature).
  The Privacy Notice (`Login.privacy_sections`) lists these fields.
- No credits screen: `Licences.publish()` prints the licence notices the shipped parts need
  (Godot's licence and third-party list read from the engine, CC0 character, fonts' OFL, also in
  assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt) to the system log at start (`Licences.text()` builds them).
- Scene flow: `Game.goto_lobby()`, `Game.goto_arena()`, `Game.goto_hud_editor()`;
  `Game.goto_login()` opens scenes/login.tscn (`Login`) until `profile.guest_ok`, the lobby after
  that and always in automated runs.
- `Login` (scripts/login.gd): GUEST LOGIN (PLAY AS GUEST, the Terms of Use and Privacy Notice as
  in-game popups, Android back = quit dialog) then CREATE PROFILE (name, picture, name card,
  preview, player ID; back returns to the guest login). CONFIRM = `Login.create_profile(name,
  avatar, banner)`: name (clean_name), `name_set`, `guest_ok`, `avatar`, `banner`, then
  `Game.save_profile()`; WELCOME, then `Game.goto_lobby()`. Shared art helpers:
  `Login.avatar_texture(id)` (the emblem or an outfit portrait's head) and
  `Login.banner_texture(id)` (`Game.BANNERS[i]` = assets/ui/banners/banner_<i+1>.png). The
  Privacy Notice (`Login.privacy_sections()`) states what cloud.gd shares (the player card's
  fields, Cloud.BROKERS hosts, the card and request ages): change it with them
  (tests/login_check.gd checks it). The player ID comes from `Cloud.prepare_identity()` (the key
  made or loaded without going online). Node names: GuestButton, TermsLink, PrivacyLink, Modal,
  ModalOk, DocScroll, QuitDialog, QuitCancel, QuitConfirm, BackButton, NameField, NameRandom,
  NameHint, Avatar_<id>, Banner_<id>, NameCard, PlayerId, GuestBadge, ConfirmButton, Welcome.
- Every button that the screenshot walkthrough (scripts/autotest.gd) uses keeps its node name:
  `StartButton`, `ModeCard`, `Mode_<mode>`, `Diff_<chip>`, `SettingsButton`, `BundlesButton`
  (WARDROBE), `Bundle_<id>`, `EquipButton`, `PlayerCard` (opens PROFILE; its pencil `EditName`,
  a 56 px touch target around its plate, the name editor), `PlayerName`,
  `Logo`, `Modal`, `PlayAgain`, `Lobby`, `Close`, `SaveButton`.
