# Lone Wolf Arena: style and feel spec

Measurable targets for the game, distilled from private reference research (facts in our own
words; the reference screenshots themselves are never stored in the repo). Everything here is
implemented with ORIGINAL assets and names. Positions are fractions of screen width/height
(x, y of the centre) on a landscape phone; sizes are fractions of screen height.
QA agents (`tools/agents/agents/visual-qa.txt`, `gameplay-tester.txt`) check against this file.

## 1. Mode and round flow (reference: 1v1 "Lone Wolf" duels)
| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Modes | Two, both 1 vs 1 on the one map CARGO CAGE: TRAINING GROUND (against a bot, EASY / NORMAL / HARD, or the TARGET DUMMY: endless HP, no zone, the clock counts up, nothing counted) and LONE WOLF (online against another player; START opens the ONLINE list). TRAINING GROUND is the default |
| Format | First to 5 rounds (best of 9), as in the reference mode; a custom room plays first to 1-7 |
| Flow | Lobby START -> loading -> PREP 10 s (spawn alcove behind a glowing red floor barrier, red countdown, weapon pick panel) -> "ROUND 1 / AREA: CARGO CAGE" banner -> FIGHT 90 s -> next round (gun picked every 2 rounds, the pick alternates) ... -> result |
| Weapon pick | Grid of weapons, the chosen gun is used by BOTH players; 10 s; default pick if the timer ends |
| Loadout | Chosen gun + fists, 2 medkits, 2 shield walls, 1 frag grenade, Lv2 helmet + Lv2 vest |
| HP | 200, resets each round; no knock-down in 1v1 (0 HP = eliminated) |
| Zone | Holds 25 s, then shrinks over 40 s to r = 4 m; damage 5 HP/s (first 10 s outside), then 10, then 20 |
| Time-out | Higher HP wins; equal HP -> draw |
| Score HUD | Top centre: [your bar][blue box: your rounds][timer + "OBJECTIVE: 5" (the rounds to win)][orange box: enemy rounds][enemy bar]; timer red in the last 10 s and during prep |

## 2. Map "Cargo Cage" (original, warehouse duel arena)
- Enclosed industrial hall, 42 x 30 m inside, 9 m walls, 180-degree rotational symmetry, spawn
  alcoves at both short ends (red barrier during prep).
- Corrugated steel walls: ochre-orange lower band (0-2.6 m), light grey above; a row of high
  windows (bright daylight); rust-brown roof trusses with hanging fluorescent tubes.
- Grey polished concrete floor with 4 m slab joints, yellow-black hazard stripes, yellow hatched
  floor pads, dashed lane lines.
- Shipping containers in light blue, deep blue, rust red, orange and cream (20 ft and 40 ft, some
  stacked 2-high, an open walk-through container), steel ramps and crate steps up to the
  high ground: a 40 ft container on each side of an open 8.5 m centre bay (bare floor where the
  safe zone's final 4 m circle closes), X-braced wooden crates as steps/cover, barrels, pallets, a forklift, a tank,
  bollards, roll-up doors, fire cabinets, area signs (A / B).
- Lighting: neutral bright daylight + fluorescent ambience, soft shadows, vivid colours.
- Performance budget (phone): <= 220 draw calls and <= 250k primitives per frame in the arena.

## 3. In-match HUD (default layout, 16:9 .. 22:9)
Positions of the movable widgets start from the reference's default layout, measured on a 20:9
recording (no reload, scoreboard or free-look buttons there), and live in code:
`HudLayout.DEFAULTS` in scripts/hud_layout.gd (x in 20:9 widths with an edge anchor l / c / r,
so the layout keeps its look on 16:9; y and sizes in screen heights). The table quotes them on
20:9; the unit tests check that no two default touch areas overlap from 16:10 to 22:9 (the gear
on the weapon panel's corner aside). Our own decisions where it departs from the reference:
- the shield wall button sits on the left edge above the medkit (the right thumb is busy with
  FIRE, SCOPE and the jumps; the left one is free while the stick is not held);
- the settings gear sits outside the weapon panel, in the screen's top-right corner beside it;
- PRONE sits under the weapon panel, left of SCOPE: the reference's place (top, right of the
  score bar, centre-anchored) is under the right-anchored panel on screens below about 18:9;
- a LEFT FIRE button (hold to fire with the left thumb) and a FREE LOOK eye are added (the eye
  starts off, as the reference's free camera button; SETTINGS > CONTROLS turns it on);
- the reference's quick RELOAD and quick weapon SWITCH buttons start on (the owner's wish; the
  reference ships them off); each can be turned off in SETTINGS > CONTROLS, as LEFT FIRE;
- a camera cutout on a short side moves the edge-anchored widgets in by its width.

| Scoreboard | (no button) | - | a tap on the score bar opens it |
- Buttons: dark translucent circles (~35-45 % black), white glyphs, opacity per button editable.
- Crosshair: WHITE by default, a thin circle + small centre dot (fixed with four short outer ticks
  for shotguns, a dot for the sniper, a small circle for the fists); the rifles' circle widens with
  movement/firing; turns RED only when a shot would hit (the crosshair's ray meets the enemy before
  cover); red-dot reticle when aiming (ADS), full scope for the sniper.
- Damage numbers: yellow, bold, outlined, float up; headshots orange-red and larger; every shot its
  own number, the HP it really took (a shotgun shell's pellets add up, its head part a number of
  its own); numbers still showing line up side by side.
- Hit marker: white X; headshot: orange X + head icon; kill: big red X on the enemy and the
  elimination badge (our emblem) with HEADSHOT / ELIMINATED and the victim's name plate.
- Damage direction: red arc segments around the centre; low HP: red vignette pulse.
- Prep: big red countdown digits; round banner: horizontal band blue (left) fading to orange (right).

## 4. Game feel (numbers)
| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Run / sprint / walk | 4.6 / 5.5 / 1.85 m/s with a rifle, times the class in hand: fists 1.15, SMG 1.06, rifle and pistol 1.0, shotgun 0.97, marksman 0.95, sniper 0.92 (Weapons.CLASS_MOVE; sprint = joystick pushed past the ring for 0.12 s, or the sprint button, as the run mode allows; never while aiming, healing or firing) |
| Crouch / prone | 1.6 / 0.7 m/s (times the same class factor); stance change 0.2 s / 0.45 s |
| ADS / firing speed | x0.70 / firing x0.80-0.85 for ARs and shotguns (SMGs x0.93-0.95, pistols x0.90-0.92, DMR x0.75, snipers x0.60) |
| Jump | apex ~1.3 m (can climb 1.2 m crates), gravity 22 m/s2 |
| Fall damage | none up to 4 m, then 12 HP per extra metre |
| Head multiplier | pistols 4.0 (HAWK .50) / 3.4 (M1917), snipers 3.0-3.2, ARs and the DMR 2.5-2.6, SMGs 2.2, shotgun pellets 2.0-2.2, fists 1.5 (decays to 80 % at range for SMGs and shotguns); legs x0.75-0.8 (values: weapons.gd) |
| Armour | helmet Lv2 35 %, vest Lv2 35 % (Weapons.HELMET_REDUCTION / VEST_REDUCTION) |
| Medkit | +75 HP over 4 s (1 s wind-up then 25 HP/s), walk at 60 %, firing cancels |
| Shield wall | 600 HP, 3.5 x 2.0 m, lasts 45 s |
| Frag | 150 at centre -> 60 at 5 m, 3 s fuse; body damage, so the Lv2 vest takes a third off it; a frag kill reads FRAG in the kill feed |
| Aim assist | hip cone 9 deg at 10 m (x 10/d, clamped 0.6..3) out to 30 m; scoped 2.5 deg to 80 m; strength 0.9 x the weapon's assist factor (SMGs 1.15, shotguns 1.2, DMR 0.8, snipers 0.5); on target the look slows to 0.4 and follows 100 % of the target's motion; an upward drag from the chest takes fixed shares of the screen at any distance: 4 % to the head band (+-0.15 m, the head's hit zone), the head band 26 % (the stop), a 0.25 m lip 8 %, then free (x strength); a mostly upward drag keeps 35 % of its sideways part; firing pulls sideways onto the body and, once a drag stops (net drag over 3 ticks < 0.4 % of the screen, so jitter does not count), holds what the drag chose (head, from the lip too; body; or nothing over the head) against the recoil; bloom counts a quarter on target; the first shot waits up to 0.15 s for a drag that starts with the press (0.15 s more while a slight overshoot is pulled back), first bullet after a pause at half spread (values: player.gd ASSIST) |
| Aim checks (`--autotest=aim`) | pull onto body < 0.6 s; drag sweep from the chest: body, then >= 15 % of the screen on the head, then over the head; while firing: a jittering thumb and a 2 % flick stay on the body, 15 %, 30 % and slanted (22 % up, 12 % sideways) flicks end on the head, 50 % goes over; first bullet: a 12 % flick with the press = head (its line passing < 0.07 m beside the head's middle), no drag = body; on-target % with assist > without; red crosshair only on the body |

## 5. Screens
- Loading (boot, the main scene): full-bleed key art of our own character in a container yard
  at sunset (slow push-in), logo top-left, a thin gold progress bar near the bottom with the
  percentage, status text and rotating tips, the version.
- Match loading (START / an online challenge / PLAY AGAIN): key-art backdrop, the mode + map
  card (TRAINING GROUND or LONE WOLF / CARGO CAGE) with chips: FIRST TO 5, 1 VS BOT, BOT: <level>
  (or TARGET DUMMY); online FIRST TO n, 1 VS 1 ONLINE and the opponent's name, never a bot; big
  map wordmark, the bar.
- Lobby (the reference lobby's layout): character large on a lit podium in the middle of a 3D
  container yard; player card top-left (emblem avatar, level, name with EDIT, XP line) and nothing
  store-like (no currency, no featured or "new" cards); top-right the logo, the ping chip, FRIENDS
  (two busts; the unread badge) and the gear; bottom-left a dark translucent bar of main buttons
  (WARDROBE | ONLINE 1 VS 1, glyph and caption each); bottom-right the mode card (TRAINING GROUND /
  CARGO CAGE / "BOT: NORMAL · FIRST TO 5" or "TARGET DUMMY"; LONE WOLF / CARGO CAGE / "1 VS 1
  ONLINE"; a tap opens SELECT MODE: the two mode cards, TRAINING GROUND's with the opponent chips
  EASY, NORMAL, HARD, DUMMY) and the big yellow START (LONE WOLF: opens the ONLINE list). No text
  menu, stats chips, name plate, outfit or version text (CUSTOM HUD and HOW TO PLAY are in
  SETTINGS).
- Wardrobe (WARDROBE): every outfit free and unlocked (no prices, ranks or rarity tabs);
  3-column item cards with the rendered outfit portrait and the outfit's own colour bar, live
  preview on the podium, EQUIP and BACK.
- Result: mode/map text top-left (TRAINING GROUND or LONE WOLF), our medal with gold "VICTORY" /
  grey "DEFEAT" / white "DRAW" / red "INVALID", round score plates, stats table (ELIMINATIONS,
  DAMAGE, HEADSHOTS, ACCURACY, TIME, HP LEFT; the opponent's row says BOT · <level>, or ONLINE
  for a player, LEFT after they quit, with "<NAME> LEFT THE MATCH" under the table), honour tag,
  XP bar, PLAY AGAIN / LOBBY; an internet match: ADD FRIEND / REMATCH / LOBBY with a status line
  above them ("<NAME> WANTS A REMATCH").
- Crosshair sizes (radius, the reference's M1887 circle is ~0.07 h across): rifle ring 0.028 h (grows
  with the spread up to 0.06 h), shotgun ring 0.035 h fixed, fists a dot in a 0.0175 h ring; lines
  ~1.4 px with a soft shadow.
- Settings (the reference's groups and defaults, our own words; only what it offers plus what
  the owner asked for): DISPLAY (graphics AUTO / LOW .. EXTREME with AUTO's pick marked and a heat
  warning before EXTREME, 30/60 FPS plus 90/120 where the screen refreshes that fast, visual
  effects), CONTROLS (SHOOTING: aim precision ASSISTED / MANUAL IN SCOPE / FULL MANUAL, left fire
  button OFF / ALWAYS / WHEN AIMING, hold fire to scope; QUICK BUTTONS: weapon switch, reload, the
  reload ring, free look (off); MOVEMENT: joystick DYNAMIC (default) / FIXED, run mode TAP / DRAG
  / MIXED; VIBRATION: enemy footsteps on, taking damage off, shooting off; custom HUD),
  SENSITIVITY 1-200 (general, red dot, 2x scope, sniper scope, free look; presets STANDARD /
  FAST), SOUND (effects, music, voice chat), ONLINE (chat, friends, career, who can reach you,
  vibration out of a match, voice, direct connection), ACCOUNT (the player ID with COPY, CLEAR
  CACHE; along the bottom DELETE MY DATA, Terms of Use, Privacy Notice as underlined links); HOW
  TO PLAY under the tabs; per-tab RESET that asks first. No FPS counter, crosshair colour or size,
  fire-button drag or master volume: everything else is fixed at its best value.
- In-match menu (the gear, Android back): the settings at once, as the reference's gear opens
  them in a match, with QUIT MATCH (red text on a dark plate, asks again) at the foot of the tab
  column above HOW TO PLAY and RESET, and EDIT CUSTOM HUD opening the editor over the match; X resumes. TRAINING
  GROUND pauses under it, online the match runs on.
