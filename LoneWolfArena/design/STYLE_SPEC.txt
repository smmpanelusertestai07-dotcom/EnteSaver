# Lone Wolf Arena: style and feel spec

Measurable targets for the game, distilled from private reference research (facts in our own
words; the reference screenshots themselves are never stored in the repo). Everything here is
implemented with ORIGINAL assets and names. Positions are fractions of screen width/height
(x, y of the centre) on a landscape phone; sizes are fractions of screen height.
QA agents (`tools/agents/agents/visual-qa.txt`, `gameplay-tester.txt`) check against this file.

## 1. Mode and round flow (reference: 1v1 "Lone Wolf" duels)
| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Format | 1 vs 1 bot, ONE round per START (owner's choice; the reference mode is first-to-5) |
| Flow | Lobby START -> loading -> PREP 10 s (spawn alcove behind a glowing red floor barrier, red countdown, weapon pick panel) -> "ROUND 1 / AREA: CARGO CAGE" banner -> FIGHT 90 s -> result |
| Weapon pick | Grid of weapons, the chosen gun is used by BOTH players; 10 s; default pick if the timer ends |
| Loadout | Chosen gun + fists, 2 medkits, 2 shield walls, 1 frag grenade, Lv2 helmet + Lv2 vest |
| HP | 200, resets each round; no knock-down in 1v1 (0 HP = eliminated) |
| Zone | Holds 25 s, then shrinks over 40 s to r = 4 m; damage 5 HP/s (first 10 s outside), then 10, then 20 |
| Time-out | Higher HP wins; equal HP -> draw |
| Score HUD | Top centre: [your bar][blue box: your rounds][timer + "OBJECTIVE 1"][orange box: enemy rounds][enemy bar]; timer red in the last 10 s and during prep |

## 2. Map "Cargo Cage" (original, warehouse duel arena)
- Enclosed industrial hall, 42 x 30 m inside, 9 m walls, 180-degree rotational symmetry, spawn
  alcoves at both short ends (red barrier during prep).
- Corrugated steel walls: ochre-orange lower band (0-2.6 m), light grey above; a row of high
  windows (bright daylight); rust-brown roof trusses with hanging fluorescent tubes.
- Grey polished concrete floor with 4 m slab joints, yellow-black hazard stripes, yellow hatched
  floor pads, dashed lane lines.
- Shipping containers in light blue, deep blue, rust red, orange and cream (20 ft and 40 ft, some
  stacked 2-high, an open walk-through container), steel ramps and crate steps up to the
  high ground: a 40 ft container on each side of an open 8.5 m centre bay (bare floor where the
  safe zone's final 4 m circle closes), X-braced wooden crates as steps/cover, barrels, pallets, a forklift, a tank,
  bollards, roll-up doors, fire cabinets, area signs (A / B).
- Lighting: neutral bright daylight + fluorescent ambience, soft shadows, vivid colours.
- Performance budget (phone): <= 220 draw calls and <= 250k primitives per frame in the arena.

## 3. In-match HUD (default layout, 16:9 .. 20:9)

recording (no left fire, reload, scoreboard or free-look buttons), and live in code:
`HudLayout.DEFAULTS` in scripts/hud_layout.gd (x in 20:9 widths with an edge anchor, so the
layout keeps its look on 16:9; y and sizes in screen heights). The table quotes them on 20:9.

- Buttons: dark translucent circles (~35-45 % black), white glyphs, opacity per button editable.
- Crosshair: WHITE by default, a ring + centre dot (a 4-arc ring for shotguns, a dot for the
  sniper and fists) that spreads with movement/firing; turns RED when on an enemy; red-dot reticle when
  aiming (ADS), full scope for the sniper.
- Damage numbers: yellow, bold, outlined, float up; headshots orange-red and larger; hits on the
  same target within 0.32 s add up into one number, separate numbers line up side by side.
- Hit marker: white X; headshot: orange X + head icon; kill: big red X on the enemy and the
  elimination badge (our emblem) with HEADSHOT / ELIMINATED and the victim's name plate.
- Damage direction: red arc segments around the centre; low HP: red vignette pulse.
- Prep: big red countdown digits; round banner: horizontal band blue (left) fading to orange (right).

## 4. Game feel (numbers)
| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Run / sprint / walk | 5.0 / 6.0 / 2.0 m/s (sprint = joystick pushed past the ring for 0.3 s) |
| Crouch / prone | 1.75 / 0.75 m/s; stance change 0.2 s / 0.45 s |
| ADS / firing speed | x0.70 / x0.85 (SMG x0.95) |
| Jump | apex ~1.3 m (can climb 1.2 m crates), gravity 22 m/s2 |
| Fall damage | none up to 4 m, then 12 HP per extra metre |
| Head multiplier | pistols/snipers 5.5, ARs 4.0, SMGs 3.5, shotgun pellets 3.0 (decays to 80 % at range for SMG/SG) |
| Armour | helmet Lv2 45 %, vest Lv2 50 % |
| Medkit | +75 HP over 4 s (1 s wind-up then 25 HP/s), walk at 60 %, firing cancels |
| Shield wall | 600 HP, 3.5 x 2.0 m, lasts 45 s |
| Frag | 150 at centre -> 60 at 5 m, 3 s fuse; body damage, so the Lv2 vest halves it |
| Aim assist | hip cone 9 deg at 10 m (x 10/d, clamped 0.6..3) out to 30 m; scoped 2.5 deg to 80 m; strength 0.9 x the weapon's assist factor (SMG 1.15, shotguns 1.2, sniper 0.5); on target the look slows to 0.4 and follows 100 % of the target's motion; an upward drag from the chest takes fixed shares of the screen at any distance: 4 % to the head band (+-0.2 m), the head band 26 % (the stop), a 0.25 m lip 8 %, then free (x strength); a mostly upward drag keeps 35 % of its sideways part; firing pulls sideways onto the body and, once a drag stops (net drag over 3 ticks < 0.4 % of the screen, so jitter does not count), holds what the drag chose (head, from the lip too; body; or nothing over the head) against the recoil; bloom counts a quarter on target; the first shot waits up to 0.15 s for a drag that starts with the press (0.15 s more while a slight overshoot is pulled back), first bullet after a pause at half spread (values: player.gd ASSIST) |
| Aim checks (`--autotest=aim`) | pull onto body < 0.6 s; drag sweep from the chest: body, then >= 15 % of the screen on the head, then over the head; while firing: a jittering thumb and a 2 % flick stay on the body, 15 %, 30 % and slanted (22 % up, 12 % sideways) flicks end on the head, 50 % goes over; first bullet: a 12 % flick with the press = head, no drag = body; on-target % with assist > without; red crosshair only on the body |

## 5. Screens
- Loading (boot, the main scene): full-bleed key art of our own character in a container yard
  at sunset (slow push-in), logo top-left, a thin gold progress bar near the bottom with the
  percentage, status text and rotating tips, the version.
- Match loading (START / PLAY AGAIN): key-art backdrop, LONE WOLF / CARGO CAGE mode card with
  chips (ROUNDS 1, 1 VS BOT, bot difficulty), big map wordmark, the bar.
- Lobby: character large on a lit podium in a 3D container yard; top bar (emblem avatar, level,
  name with EDIT, XP line, offline coins and wins); left text menu (BUNDLES, CUSTOM HUD,
  SETTINGS, HOW TO PLAY); featured duel card top-left; right: stats chips, bot
  difficulty, mode card "LONE WOLF / CARGO CAGE" and the big yellow START bottom-right.
- Wardrobe (BUNDLES): rarity tabs, 3-column item cards with the rendered outfit portrait and a
  rarity bar, live preview on the podium, EQUIP and BACK.
- Result: mode/map text top-left, our medal with gold "VICTORY" / grey "DEFEAT" / white "DRAW" /
  red "INVALID", round score plates, stats table (ELIMINATIONS, DAMAGE, HEADSHOTS, ACCURACY,
  TIME, HP LEFT), honour tag, XP bar, PLAY AGAIN / LOBBY.
- Crosshair: small like the reference: rifle ring 0.019 h (grows with the spread up to 0.055 h),
  shotgun ring 0.027 h (to 0.08 h), fists a dot in a 0.015 h ring; red on an enemy.
- Settings (only what the reference mode offers): BASIC (graphics LOW .. EXTREME, 30/60/90 FPS),
  CONTROLS (dynamic joystick, custom HUD), SENSITIVITY (general, red dot, sniper scope,
  fire-button drag), SOUND; per-tab RESET. Everything else is fixed at its best value.
- Pause: full-width dark band, PAUSED, score / time left / HP chips, RESUME, SETTINGS, QUIT MATCH
  (asks again).
